About

Italy → Europe

I started in visual communication and moved into digital marketing, where I learned to connect creative work with commercial results. Over the past three years I have managed paid media, CRM, content and partnerships for a 13-property hospitality group, helping grow the share of online revenue from direct bookings from 20% to 70%.

I am now looking for a growth, CRM or lifecycle role where I can work with first-party customer data, experimentation and retention across the full journey. I bring a practical habit with me: start from the metric, write down the hypothesis and report what did not work as clearly as what did.

How I work

  • Start from the metric that is actually broken, with its starting value.
  • Write the hypothesis down before acting: changing X to Y will move Z, because…
  • Keep a holdout, even a small one, so the result means something.
  • Report what did not work with the same detail as what did.
