The route here

I did not arrive at marketing through a business degree. I spent my first years at art school in Cuneo, learning why one image holds a person for three seconds while the one next to it does not. Then a master's in Milan, where the question changed: what happens to that attention once a company needs it to become a decision.

Someone watches a video on a Tuesday night in February and sleeps in a room in July. Between those two moments there are a hundred small decisions you can either guess at or measure.

How I work
  • Start from the metric that is actually broken, with its starting value.
  • Write the hypothesis down before acting: changing X to Y will move Z, because…
  • Keep a holdout, even a small one, so the result means something.
  • Report what did not work with the same detail as what did.
