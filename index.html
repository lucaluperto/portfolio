about.

I did not arrive at marketing through a business degree. I spent my first years at art school in Cuneo, learning why one image holds a person for three seconds while the one next to it does not. Then a master's in Milan, where the question changed: what happens to that attention once a company needs it to become a decision.

How I work
  1. 01Start from the metric that is actually broken, with its starting value.
  2. 02Write the hypothesis down before acting: changing X to Y will move Z, because…
  3. 03Keep a holdout, even a small one, so the result means something.
  4. 04Report what did not work with the same detail as what did.
